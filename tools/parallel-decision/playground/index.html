Camera frames stay on this machine; live frames are not kept in the history.
Tool router: a decision picks the tool and every argument with a fixed set of values (enums, booleans, small integer ranges), with probabilities, in one pass. Only arguments that are free text are then written by a short chat call for the chosen tool. Below the threshold the page asks back instead of calling.
Body photo check: one photo of one person, head to feet. The page decides whether it is fit for measuring, and the model estimates sizes in cm with a p10-p90 range, or "none" where the photo does not show them. These are model estimates, not measurements: a single photo has no scale, so treat them as rough until calibrated on measured people.
Calibration from my measurements (0 samples)
Measure a person with a tape, take the photo, press Decide, then enter the true sizes and add the sample. Only the model output and your numbers are kept (in this browser, and in the export), never the photo.
Schema
For a CPU: compact ranges shorten the prompt a lot; greedy or pruning then need fewer rows. Exact tree probabilities are cheap on a GPU.
Images are scaled down in the browser before sending. Big photos cost many image tokens and take minutes on a CPU.
